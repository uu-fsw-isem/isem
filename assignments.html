Assignments

This chapter contains the details and binding information about the two data analysis assignments upon which your course grade is based.

Below, you can find a brief idea of what each assignment will cover.

  • For each assignment, you will use R to analyze some real-world data.
  • You will report your findings via standard methods of scientific communication.
  • You will complete the Assignment 1 individually.
  • You will complete Assignment 2 in the Assignment Group that you join on Brightspace.
  • The first assignment is graded as pass/fail.
  • You must pass this first assignment to pass the course.
  • Your grade on the second assignment constitutes your numeric course grade.
