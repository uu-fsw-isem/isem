Note on Data Updates

During the course, we may need to update some of these datasets and/or add some new datasets to the SurfDrive directory. If so, you will need to download the updated data.

We will let you know if and when any datasets are modified. In such situations, you are responsible for updating your data.

  • Working with outdated data will probably produce incorrect results.
    • Your answer won’t match the solutions we expect.
    • Your answer will be marked as incorrect, even if the code used to produce the answer is correct.
    • Points lost on an assignment due to using outdated datasets will not be returned.
